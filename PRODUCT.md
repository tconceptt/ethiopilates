# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in Addis Ababa seeking a premium, women-only Pilates and wellness studio — beginners, expecting mothers, and experienced movers. They arrive from Instagram or word of mouth, mostly on mobile, deciding whether the studio feels like *their* place before committing to a package. Booking happens on-site via a Telebirr advance-payment flow.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site + class booking for Ethio Pilates Studio. It must communicate what the physical studio already delivers: a serene, luxurious, women-first sanctuary (reformer hall, mat studio, sauna, ice bath, red light therapy, locker lounge). Success = a visitor trusts the studio enough to reserve a class or buy a package.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, quietly empowering. The studio's own walls say it: "Who run the world — GIRLS" in brass letters, and "You are exactly where you need to be." The voice is a confident exhale, not a shout. Motto: **Move · Breathe · Thrive.**

## Anti-references

- Loud fitness-gym sites: neon gradients, countdown timers, "CRUSH YOUR GOALS" copy.
- Generic AI landing pages: cream-and-serif templates with no real imagery, identical card grids, eyebrow labels over every section.
- Sterile spa minimalism that hides the actual space behind stock photos.

## Design Principles

1. **The studio is the design.** Real photography of the space (brass wall type, oxblood lockers, olive walls, warm oak, black reformers) carries the site; layout and type frame it.
2. **Calm is pacing, not emptiness.** Generous spacing, slow confident motion, one idea per fold — never sensory overload, never bare.
3. **Warm materials, not warm defaults.** Color comes from the building's own materials (brass, oxblood, olive, espresso), not from a template cream.
4. **Empowerment in the details.** Copy and imagery celebrate women's strength; the brand's wall quotes are part of the voice.
5. **Booking is sacred.** Every page ends in a clear, friction-light path to reserving a class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Mobile-first responsiveness (majority of traffic). Amharic phone formats and ETB pricing conventions respected.
